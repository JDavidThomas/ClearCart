---
name: NewsAPI developer plan
description: NewsAPI.org's free Developer plan limits for food-recall news search.
---

NewsAPI.org's free Developer plan is limited to development and testing, has a 24-hour article delay, supports searches up to one month old, and allows 100 requests per day. It cannot be used in staging or production; a subscription is required outside development.

**Why:** The food-recall app's news layer may be published later, and the free key's permitted use and delay affect what can safely ship.

**How to apply:** A free key is suitable for local/Replit development verification of the 14-day search, but switch to an eligible production plan before enabling NewsAPI in a published app.
